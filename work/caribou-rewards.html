Overview

Caribou Rewards brings Caribou Coffee's ordering and loyalty experience to Android, delivered for Arabian Coffee FZE on the COFE Cloud white-label platform. The same shared codebase that powers Barns and FiLLi is themed and configured here into a distinct, fully branded Caribou app.
