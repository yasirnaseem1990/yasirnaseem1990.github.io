Overview

Barns is a leading Saudi coffee brand. Its Android app was delivered on COFE Cloud, the white-label platform I help build and maintain, where changing theme and configuration attributes produces a fully branded application from a single shared codebase. Barns is the platform's flagship proof point: a top-rated national brand app powered by the same engine that ships multiple other brands.

What I Built

  • Delivered the Barns branded Android app from the shared COFE Cloud codebase, theming UI, flows, and configuration to the brand.
  • Implemented mobile ordering, store locator, loyalty and rewards, and secure checkout.
