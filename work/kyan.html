Overview

Kyan is a coffee brand app delivered on the COFE Cloud white-label platform for Quwa Kayan Trading. The same shared codebase that powers Barns, FiLLi and Caribou is themed and configured here into a fully branded Kyan experience, with ordering, a wallet, and gamified rewards.
